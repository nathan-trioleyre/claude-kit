- Angular 22 (TypeScript 6 supported): Signal Forms, `resource`/`httpResource` and Angular Aria are stable; prefer them for new code.
- Prefer the `@Service` decorator over `@Injectable({ providedIn: 'root' })` for new root services; use `injectAsync` to lazy-load heavy services.
- Templates: `@switch` accepts multiple cases and flags unhandled union members at compile time; keep `@switch` exhaustive.
- Host directives are de-duplicated: an input/output exposed under several names now throws.
- `*ngIf`/`*ngFor`/`*ngSwitch` were deprecated in v20 and may be removed: use built-in control flow only.
- Router follows the browser Navigation API; `withExperimentalAutoCleanupInjectors` is experimental, don't rely on it by default.
